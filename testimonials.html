More from clients

“I had never heard of The Grinberg Method until I met Sam, and he made me feel safe and seen throughout our session. Sam is strong and gentle at the same time, and sensitive not only with his touch but also with his presence. I experienced a deep release, which didn’t make a lot of rational sense to my thinking mind, but left my body and my being feeling lighter and cleaner somehow. I would definitely have another session with Sam and have no hesitation in recommending him. If you’re curious, go for it!”

Ola, Actress — London

“I’ve benefited a lot from Samuel’s therapy. In particular, he’s helped me with connecting my body to my breath. One of the unexpected benefits I gained is that it helped me uncover unconscious beliefs and patterns that were holding me back. I also feel a sense of relief after leaving his sessions — physically and emotionally lighter — and would recommend him to anyone who is open to participating in a mixed approach to therapy.”

Robyn — Hove

“Samuel creates a respectful, compassionate and safe space, something that feels especially important when working with trauma. He has a real gift of being fully present during the sessions — insightful and tuned in, making observations about something quite subtle, sometimes before I’m even aware of it myself. During our time together, I’ve had some big insights into just how much past traumas are still being held in my body. Samuel’s skill in working sensitively with trauma is evident. I wholeheartedly recommend Samuel for the Grinberg Method and have already recommended him to my friends.”

Jo — Brighton

“I would highly recommend Samuel to anyone experiencing stress or anxiety. Samuel is really kind and creates a calm and safe atmosphere. The sessions helped me to focus on my breathing and use my breath to calm nerves or anxiety. It helped me understand more deeply how we embody stress — and how, by focusing on the breath and the body, we can reduce the feeling of stress in the mind. I always left feeling stronger, calmer, and with more clarity of thought, and most of all, better at listening to my body.”

Maria — Hove

I wholeheartedly recommend Samuel.”
