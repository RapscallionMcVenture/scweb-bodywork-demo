About

Samuel Morgan

I began training as a bodywork therapist in 2017, qualifying in the Bowen Technique in 2020 and the Grinberg Method™ in 2023. That journey was driven by my own first-hand experience of what bodywork can do — how it helped me become more grounded, less dominated by my thinking mind, and better equipped to navigate pain and emotional difficulty.

I bring sensitivity, curiosity and care to my work with clients. I listen deeply and try to meet each person where they actually are — not where I think they should be. My warmth and calmness are genuine; so is my commitment to doing work that actually helps.

I work from a clinic in London. Sessions are available Monday–Thursday 4–7pm, Friday all day, and Saturday mornings.

“The body is constantly communicating. My job is to help you hear it.”
