A different approach

Your Body Knows.

Many of us spend years repeating the same reactions — in how we hold tension, how we respond to stress, how we brace, push, or withdraw. Over time, the range of what feels possible begins to shrink.

Bodywork therapy can help you become aware of those patterns and learn, through the body, how to do something different — so you can live with greater ease, calm, and connection to yourself.

Less holding. More living.

Who this is for

This work may help
if you're dealing with:

  • Chronic tension or recurring pain
  • Stress, anxiety or difficulty switching off
  • Emotional overwhelm or feeling stuck
  • Difficulty relaxing, even when you want to
  • Feeling disconnected from your body
  • A sense of walking in circles
The Grinberg Method

This is not passive massage.

More active than massage, and more body-based than talking therapy, the Grinberg Method is a guided process that helps you notice and change patterns held in the body — through touch, breath, movement and awareness.

Not something done to you. Something you learn.
